How we work

  • Site consultation

    Ninety minutes on site. A written summary within a week, and an honest view of what your budget will actually buy.

    $180

  • Garden design

    Survey, concept, planting plan and construction drawings. Six to ten weeks, and you own the drawings whether you build with us or not.

    From $3,500

  • Build

    Stone, timber, steel, drainage and planting, by our own team. Quoted from the drawings — never from a photograph.

    Quoted

  • Establishment care

    Two seasons of aftercare on every build, then a written handover plan for whoever maintains it next.

    Included
