Services

Four things, done properly.

We do not offer a maintenance round, a tree service or a paving-only package. What follows is the whole list.

01 — $180

Site consultation

Ninety minutes on site, with you, walking the space.

We talk about how you actually use the garden, what you want from it and what the budget is. You get a written summary within a week — what we would do, roughly what it costs, and anything we think is a bad idea. The fee comes off the design if you go ahead.

02 — From $3,500

Garden design

Survey, concept, planting plan, construction drawings.

Six to ten weeks depending on survey. You see a concept before anything is drawn in detail, and nothing goes to construction drawings until you are happy with the concept. The drawings are yours whether you build with us or take them elsewhere.

03 — Quoted from drawings

Build

Stone, timber, steel, drainage and planting — our own team.

We bring in specialists only for electrical work and anything that needs an engineer's stamp. Three to four weeks on site for a terrace and planting; eight to twelve for a full garden with structures. We never quote a build from a photograph.

04 — Included

Establishment care

Two seasons, because that is what decides whether planting takes.

Watering regime, formative pruning, and replacing anything that fails in the first two years at our cost. At the end you get a written handover plan for whoever maintains it next, and we come back twice a year for the structural pruning if you want us to.
