Studio

Eleven people, one site at a time.

We take on about a dozen gardens a year. That is the number we can be on site for, and we would rather turn work away than send a crew we have never met.

The drawings are made by the people who build them.

It sounds like a small thing. It is the whole difference.

A designer who has never set a coping stone draws details that cannot be built, and a contractor who was handed those details builds something else instead. Nobody tells the client. The garden ends up a compromise between two people who never met.

Maren draws and sets out. Tomas runs the build and has laid every wall we have put up since 2014. The planting is Ruth's, and she is on site for every day of it because plants do not go in where the plan says, they go in where the light is.
