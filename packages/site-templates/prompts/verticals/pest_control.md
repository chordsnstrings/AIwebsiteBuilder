# Vertical — Pest control

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `callout_request`
- Pricing posture: `callout_fee`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, service_area, about, emergency, contact

## Design register

Discreet. This is bought with embarrassment, so the page must be calm,
clinical and completely un-lurid. A quiet humanist sans, moderate scale, plenty of
air. Palette: soft neutral paper, warm dark ink, brand colour used sparingly.
⛔ No photographs of pests anywhere. No alarming iconography. No red.

## Photography brief

1. A clean, ordinary domestic exterior — the house, not the problem.
2. A technician's kit laid out neatly, no branding, no chemicals visible.
3. A calm, tidy kitchen or loft space, well-lit — the outcome.
4. A wide residential street, unremarkable and reassuring.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. What is your callout charge?
2. What areas do you cover?
3. How soon can someone come out?
4. Do you handle commercial premises?
5. Do you offer follow-up visits?
6. How do I arrange a visit?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **treatment_claim** — triggers: safe for children, non-toxic, guaranteed eradication
  Reason: Treatment safety and efficacy claims are regulated

## Notes that change the build

⛔ The strongest copy constraint of any vertical. Never state that a
treatment is safe for children or pets, never say non-toxic, never guarantee
eradication — all three are regulated claims. Discretion is the value proposition:
say plainly that visits are unmarked if that is a supplied fact, and not otherwise.
