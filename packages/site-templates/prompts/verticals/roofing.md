# Vertical — Roofing

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `quote_request`
- Pricing posture: `never_published`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, service_area, gallery, about, emergency, contact

## Design register

Sturdy and high-contrast. A condensed grotesk or a slab for display,
a plain grotesk for text. This trade is bought under stress — after a storm, with
water coming in — so the page reads as competence, not craft.
Palette leans storm: deep slate ink, cool grey paper, the brand colour as the one
warm signal.

## Photography brief

1. A finished roof in raking late light, low angle, ridge line against sky.
2. A crew member on a roof at work, seen from behind or above — no identifiable face.
3. Close detail: new architectural shingles or a lead valley, sharp and clean.
4. A wide street-level shot of a finished house — the result the customer buys.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. Do you do emergency repairs?
2. What areas do you cover?
3. Do you work with insurance claims?
4. How long does a roof replacement take?
5. Do you offer a guarantee on the work?
6. How do I get a quote?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **trade_certification** — triggers: gas safe, niceic, part p, asbestos, working at height
  Reason: Trade certification claims are never asserted without a verified fact
- **photo_price** — triggers: from this photo, based on the picture
  Reason: The agent never prices from a photo — the owner does

## Notes that change the build

Storm and emergency are the demand trigger. Put a phone line directly under
the question box, labelled for urgent damage — a visitor with water coming in must not
have to read a whole page. It sits BESIDE the ask, never replacing it.
⛔ This vertical publishes no prices. Every price question must resolve to a refusal
or to a quote-request, never to a figure.
