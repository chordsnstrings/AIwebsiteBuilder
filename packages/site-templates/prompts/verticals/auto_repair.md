# Vertical — Auto repair

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `instant_book`
- Pricing posture: `menu_pricing`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, about, contact

## Design register

Bold and transparent. A strong grotesk, heavy display weights, tabular
figures throughout. Industrial but clean — this vertical publishes real prices, so
the page is built around a legible price menu rather than around photography.
Palette: high contrast, dark ink on light paper, the brand colour carrying the
booking action. Confident, not slick.

## Photography brief

1. A clean workshop bay, well-lit, a car on a lift.
2. Hands with a tool on an engine component, cropped, no face.
3. A row of finished cars or a tidy forecourt.
4. A detail: diagnostic screen, torque wrench, new part in its box.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. How much is an MOT?
2. Do you do servicing?
3. Can I book online?
4. What are your opening hours?
5. Do you offer courtesy cars?
6. How much is a diagnostic?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **diagnosis_claim** — triggers: it is definitely, the problem is, you need a new
  Reason: Never diagnose without inspection

## Notes that change the build

⛔ The ONLY vertical that publishes prices openly. The menu is the centre of
the page: a legible, scannable price list, tabular figures, aligned decimals.
Instant booking is available — the booking action is prominent alongside the ask.
⛔ Never diagnose. 'It is definitely the alternator' is a refusal, always: a
diagnosis without inspection is a claim the business cannot stand behind.
