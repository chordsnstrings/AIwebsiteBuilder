## Design register

Editorial and photography-led — the closest of any vertical to a design
studio's own site. A high-contrast display serif over a clean grotesk. Warm paper,
near-black ink with a green cast, restrained accent. The photographs carry the
emotion; the type stays quiet and the whitespace is generous.

## Photography brief

1. A designed garden in low evening light, wide, showing structure.
2. A path or terrace receding into planting, portrait, atmospheric.
3. A built element — wall, steps, timber structure — straight on and architectural.
4. A macro detail: seed heads, frost, stone texture, with negative space for type.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. What areas do you cover?
2. What does a garden design cost?
3. How long does a project take?
4. Do you do the building as well as the design?
5. Do you maintain gardens afterwards?
6. How do we start?
