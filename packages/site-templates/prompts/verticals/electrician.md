# Vertical — Electrician

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `callout_request`
- Pricing posture: `callout_plus_hourly`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, service_area, about, emergency, contact

## Design register

Precise and technical. A geometric or neo-grotesk display, tabular
figures in any table. Tight, engineered spacing on a visible grid.
Palette: graphite ink, cool paper, brand colour as a single amber-or-whatever
signal on actions. The look should suggest someone who measures things.

## Photography brief

1. A consumer unit, newly installed, labelled and closed — order made visible.
2. A lighting scheme at dusk in a finished interior.
3. Close detail: terminated cable in a back box, neat and correct.
4. An EV charge point on a house wall, wide enough to read the context.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. Are you registered for certification work?
2. Do you do EV chargers?
3. What is your callout charge?
4. What areas do you cover?
5. Do you handle emergency faults?
6. Can you do a full rewire?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **electrical_certification** — triggers: niceic, part p, napit, elecsa, 18th edition
  Reason: Electrical certification claims are never asserted without a verified fact
- **diy_guidance** — triggers: can i do it myself, how do i wire, is it safe to
  Reason: Never give electrical work guidance — safety-critical

## Notes that change the build

⛔ Two hard refusals with real consequences. Never state a scheme
registration that is not a supplied verified fact. Never answer 'can I do this
myself' with anything but a refusal — a DIY answer here is a safety liability.
