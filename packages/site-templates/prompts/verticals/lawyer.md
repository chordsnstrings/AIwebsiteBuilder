# Vertical — Lawyer

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `consultation_slot`
- Pricing posture: `never_published`
- Photo triage offered: no — do not offer it
- Site modules: hero, services, credentials, about, contact

## Design register

The most restrained of all. A classical text serif, moderate scale, wide
margins, strong horizontal rules. Nothing expressive. Palette: deep ink — navy or
near-black — on warm paper, one accent used almost nowhere.
⛔ No photography of people. No gavels, no scales, no columns, no library shelves.
Typography and structure only; the seriousness comes from restraint.

## Photography brief

Architectural or abstract only, and sparingly: a building facade, an
interior with strong light, a texture. Building the page without photography at all
is a legitimate answer and often the stronger one.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. What areas of law do you practise?
2. Do you offer a first consultation?
3. Where are you based?
4. How do I make an appointment?
5. Do you handle commercial matters?
6. What happens at a first meeting?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **legal_advice** — triggers: do i have a case, should i sue, am i liable, legal advice
  Reason: Never give legal advice — this is regulated
- **case_outcome** — triggers: we will win, you will win, guaranteed outcome
  Reason: Never predict a case outcome
- **fee_quote** — triggers: how much will it cost, what are your fees
  Reason: Fees are never published for this vertical

## Notes that change the build

⛔ The heaviest refusal set in the catalogue, and every one of them is a
regulatory exposure. Fees are NEVER published — a price question always refuses and
routes to a consultation. Any question about the visitor's own situation is legal
advice and refuses. Never predict an outcome, in any form, however hedged.
