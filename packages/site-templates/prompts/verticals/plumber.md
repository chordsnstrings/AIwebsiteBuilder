# Vertical — Plumber

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `callout_request`
- Pricing posture: `callout_plus_hourly`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, service_area, about, emergency, contact

## Design register

Clean and utilitarian, close to the tone of good municipal signage.
A neutral grotesk throughout, one weight step for display. Nothing decorative.
Palette: blue-grey ink, near-white paper, brand colour on the callout action only.
Generous whitespace is the luxury signal here, not ornament.

## Photography brief

1. A copper or plastic pipe run, newly installed, clean and square.
2. A van and tools at a doorstep — the arrival moment.
3. A bathroom or kitchen finished to a high standard, wide.
4. Close detail of a fitted valve or boiler flue, precise, well-lit.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. Do you do emergency callouts?
2. What is your callout charge?
3. What areas do you cover?
4. Do you fix boilers?
5. How quickly can someone come out?
6. Do you work weekends?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **gas_certification** — triggers: gas safe, corgi, gas certified
  Reason: Gas certification claims are never asserted without a verified fact

## Notes that change the build

Callout fee and hourly rate ARE published — state them plainly and early.
Transparency about the callout charge is this trade's biggest trust lever.
⛔ Never assert Gas Safe registration unless it is a supplied verified fact.
