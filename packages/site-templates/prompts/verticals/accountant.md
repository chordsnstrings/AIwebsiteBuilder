# Vertical — Accountant

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `consultation_slot`
- Pricing posture: `fee_structure_only`
- Photo triage offered: no — do not offer it
- Site modules: hero, services, credentials, about, contact

## Design register

Quiet authority. A text serif with real tabular figures for any numbers,
paired with a neutral grotesk for interface. Restrained, generously leaded, almost
bookish. Palette: warm off-white paper, deep neutral ink, brand colour on one action.
⛔ No photography of stock businesspeople. This vertical has a `credentials` module
and NO photo triage — the visual weight comes from typography and structure, not images.

## Photography brief

Use imagery sparingly and abstractly: an interior with good light, a desk
detail, a building exterior. If no suitable photography exists, build the page on
typography and rules alone — that is a legitimate and often better answer here.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. What are your fees?
2. Do you work with limited companies?
3. Can you handle self-assessment?
4. Are you a chartered practice?
5. How do we get started?
6. Do you offer a first meeting?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **tax_advice** — triggers: should i claim, can i deduct, will i owe, tax advice
  Reason: Never give tax advice — this is regulated
- **outcome_prediction** — triggers: you will get, guaranteed refund, will save you
  Reason: Never predict an outcome

## Notes that change the build

⛔ Two regulated refusals. Any question shaped as 'should I claim', 'can I
deduct', 'will I owe' is tax advice and must be refused and routed to a consultation
— even when the answer seems obvious. Never predict a refund or a saving.
Fee STRUCTURE may be published (fixed fee, monthly, hourly); specific fees may not.
The credentials module is prominent: professional body membership is the trust
signal, and it may only show supplied verified facts.
