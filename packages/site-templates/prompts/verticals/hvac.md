# Vertical — HVAC

Merged with `design-contract.md` and the business facts to produce one brief.
Nothing here overrides the contract; where they appear to conflict, the contract
wins and you should say so rather than silently choosing.

## Shape of the business

- Booking model: `quote_plus_service_slot`
- Pricing posture: `service_plans`
- Photo triage offered: yes
- Site modules: hero, services, service_area, about, emergency, contact

## Design register

Airy and seasonal. A humanist sans for display, generous line height.
The palette moves through temperature: cool paper, cool-grey ink, the brand colour
warm on actions — comfort is the product, so the page should feel comfortable.
Avoid anything that reads clinical or industrial.

## Photography brief

1. A wall-mounted indoor unit in a calm, well-lit living room.
2. An outdoor condenser on a neat plinth, planting around it, no clutter.
3. Ductwork or a heat pump install, clean and square.
4. A wide interior in strong summer light — the comfort the system buys.

## Suggested questions for the hero

Use these unless the supplied facts make one of them unanswerable, in which case
drop it — a suggested question the agent then refuses is a bad first impression.

1. Do you offer service plans?
2. Do you install heat pumps?
3. What areas do you cover?
4. How often should a system be serviced?
5. Do you handle emergency breakdowns?
6. How do I get a quote?

## Refusals specific to this vertical

These sit ON TOP of the universal set (unverified credentials, unpublished
prices, competitor comparisons, payment details in conversation, arrival-time
guarantees).

- **refrigerant_certification** — triggers: f-gas, epa 608, refrigerant certified
  Reason: Refrigerant handling certification is never asserted without a verified fact

## Notes that change the build

Service plans are the published pricing — lead with the plan tiers, laid
out as a comparison the visitor can read at a glance. Seasonal demand is real:
the page should work equally in a heatwave and a cold snap, so do not theme it
to one season.
