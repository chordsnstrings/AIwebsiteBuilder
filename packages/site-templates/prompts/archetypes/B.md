# Archetype B — Booked personal services

*Recognise it by:* Recurring appointments against chair, room or practitioner capacity
*Primary lever:* Reminders and rebooking

The visitor knows what they want and is choosing between three places that all look the same.

## The register

- Show real availability or say plainly that you will confirm. A fake slot is worse than none.
- Name the practitioner where the business names them — people rebook with a person, not a salon.
- Cancellation terms are stated once, in plain words, before booking. Never in a footer.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype B. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
