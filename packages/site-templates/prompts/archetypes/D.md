# Archetype D — Project trades

*Recognise it by:* Quoted work, a site visit, a multi-week cycle
*Primary lever:* Quote follow-up

The decision takes weeks and is made by two people who disagree about the budget.

## The register

- Show finished work at the scale the buyer is buying. One bathroom is not a testimonial for a house.
- Be explicit about what a quote includes and what it does not. Every dispute in this trade is a scope dispute.
- A site visit is the conversion event. Everything on the page exists to book one.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype D. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
