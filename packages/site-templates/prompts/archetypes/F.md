# Archetype F — Hospitality and venues

*Recognise it by:* Covers, rooms, events and deposits
*Primary lever:* Response speed on high-value enquiries

Half the enquiries are worth ten times the other half and arrive looking identical.

## The register

- Photography is the product. Give it room and never crop a room to a banner.
- State deposit and cancellation terms before the enquiry form, not after.
- Dietary, access and licensing facts are published facts — answer them exactly or not at all.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype F. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
