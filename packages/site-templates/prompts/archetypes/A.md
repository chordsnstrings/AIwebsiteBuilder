# Archetype A — Emergency trades

*Recognise it by:* Urgent, unplanned, out-of-hours demand
*Primary lever:* Speed to lead — under sixty seconds

Someone is standing in water, or in the dark, or locked out. They are not browsing.

## The register

- Lead with what you can do TODAY and how fast. Everything else is secondary.
- State the call-out charge if it is published. A hidden call-out fee at 2am is the commonest complaint in this trade.
- Never promise an arrival time. 'Same day where we safely can' is honest; 'within the hour' is a claim the business cannot keep and we cannot make for them.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype A. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
