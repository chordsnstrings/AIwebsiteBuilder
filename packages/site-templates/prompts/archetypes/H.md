# Archetype H — Education and training

*Recognise it by:* Cohorts, terms and enrolment cycles
*Primary lever:* Trial to paid, and the term break

The buyer is often not the learner, and the deadline is a term date nobody moves.

## The register

- Dates, durations and start points are the whole page. Vagueness reads as an unfinished course.
- Outcomes only where they are measured and published. 'Our students go on to great things' is nothing.
- Say who it is for AND who it is not for. The second one sells better.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype H. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
