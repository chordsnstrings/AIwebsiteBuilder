# Archetype J — Retail, product-led

*Recognise it by:* Walk-in trade with stock and no client record
*Primary lever:* Availability only

The catalogue itself marks this a weak fit and says to deprioritise it. Build accordingly: answer availability and location, and do not pretend there is a transaction to capture.

## The register

- Opening hours, location and parking are the page. That is genuinely what people came for.
- ⛔ Never state stock levels. They are wrong within the hour and a wrong 'in stock' brings someone across a city for nothing.
- No booking flow. There is nothing to book.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype J. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
