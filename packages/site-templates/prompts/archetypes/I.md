# Archetype I — Property and lettings

*Recognise it by:* Viewings, tenancies and statutory dates
*Primary lever:* Statutory dates and viewing speed

Regulated, deadline-driven, and every party assumes the other one is handling compliance.

## The register

- Fees displayed exactly as the regulator prescribes. This is a legal display, not copy.
- ⛔ Never state a property's compliance status — EPC, gas safety, licensing — from anything but a published record.
- Viewing speed is the conversion event. Make booking one the shortest path on the page.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype I. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
