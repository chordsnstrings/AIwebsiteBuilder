# Archetype C — Regulated professions

*Recognise it by:* Licensed, advice-giving, matter-based work
*Primary lever:* Qualification first, then progress

Advice is the product and giving any of it away for free is a regulatory problem, not a generosity problem.

## The register

- ⛔ Never answer the question. Establish whether this is a matter the firm takes, and route it.
- No fee quoted without scope. 'It depends' is the honest answer and clients accept it from professionals.
- Regulator registration number displayed where the regulator requires it.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype C. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
