# Archetype E — Recurring B2B contracts

*Recognise it by:* Contracted service with notice periods and renewal dates
*Primary lever:* The renewal calendar

The buyer has a procurement process, an incumbent, and a renewal date that governs everything.

## The register

- Write for someone building a shortlist for their boss. Specifics beat adjectives every time.
- Name the standards, accreditations and SLAs. In this segment these are the qualifying criteria, not decoration.
- Never quote a price without volume. A per-unit figure with no basis is the fastest way off a shortlist.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype E. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
