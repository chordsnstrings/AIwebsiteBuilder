# Archetype G — Clinical and health

*Recognise it by:* Recall intervals and treatment plans
*Primary lever:* Recall and reactivation

⛔ The highest-risk register in the catalogue. A clinical claim we generate is a claim the practice made.

## The register

- ⛔ Never describe a treatment as safe, effective, painless or guaranteed.
- ⛔ Never suggest what a symptom might be. Not once, not hedged, not 'it could be'.
- Prices only where the practice publishes them, with the 'from' exactly as they wrote it.
- Practitioner registration numbers where the regulator requires them.

## Where this applies

This is the SHARED register for archetype G. It is used by every trade in
the archetype that has no register of its own — 145 trades exist and nine have
hand-written prose, so most sites are built from this file.

⛔ A trade-specific register OVERRIDES this one entirely rather than adding to
it. Two registers merged produce a voice with two opinions about pricing, which
reads worse than either.
